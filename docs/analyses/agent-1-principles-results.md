# Agent 1 results — principles map

## Scope examined

The agent read the external collection used as research material in full,
including the 101 principles, their thematic organization, supporting documents,
and navigation structure. The count confirmed 101 distinct units distributed
across ten groups.

## Ideas worth preserving

- progressive loading by problem, without dumping the entire catalog into context;
- small, searchable units that can be applied independently;
- a connection between each principle, the situation in which it applies, the practical decision, and the evidence;
- organization by recognizable stages of product work;
- application examples kept separate from the core rule.

## What needed improvement

- several recommendations depended on context but appeared universal;
- some commercial claims presented stronger causality than the evidence supported;
- acquisition, messaging, activation, and monetization overlapped;
- the structure did not distinguish requirements, recommended defaults, and hypotheses;
- risks involving manipulation, privacy, accessibility, and autonomy did not form a
  consistent boundary across all chapters;
- text, illustrations, and sources were coupled, making selective use difficult.

## Decision incorporated

An independent catalog was created with IDs `ADO-001` through `ADO-101`. Each entry
has exactly five fields:

1. `Strength`;
2. `Apply when`;
3. `Decision`;
4. `Evidence`;
5. `Avoid`.

The strengths `required`, `default`, and `heuristic` make the degree of permitted
variation explicit for each rule. The method limits selection to three principles
per decision to avoid an automatic checklist and requires facts, hypotheses, and
preferences to remain distinct.

## Coverage produced by the agent

- `ADO-021` through `ADO-032`: acquisition, proposition, and entry pages;
- `ADO-033` through `ADO-045`: onboarding, activation, and first value.

Both modules received a summary, continuous IDs, complete fields, and original
wording. No example, name, link, or external asset was carried over.
