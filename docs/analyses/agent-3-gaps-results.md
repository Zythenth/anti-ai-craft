# Agent 3 results — gaps in the design skill

## Previous state

The existing skill was already strong in project inspection, visual direction,
identity preservation, information architecture, tokens, accessibility,
responsiveness, states, media, evidence-based review, and prevention of generic
interfaces.

## Confirmed gaps

It lacked a sufficiently explicit contract for:

- reconciling the user's outcome with the organization's outcome;
- positioning a proposition without turning copy into an invented promise;
- structuring acquisition, the above-the-fold area, evidence, and the call to action;
- defining activation and first value;
- evaluating feature discovery, adoption, and lifecycle;
- designing plans, billing, limits, and upgrades with consent;
- distinguishing healthy retention from lock-in;
- defining guardrail metrics, disconfirming evidence, and a reversal condition;
- differentiating AI-accelerated products through real work rather than decoration.

## Architectural decision

No new skill was created. The gaps belong to the scoped interface domain already
served by `$anti-ai-design`. The catalog was placed in thematic references, and
the primary contract routes directly to each file. This improves discovery of the
skill without turning every visual request into commercial strategy.

## Consistency improvements

During synthesis, duplication among strategy, dashboards, product changes, and
features was removed. The catalog now distinguishes:

- a feature's ongoing cost and the definition of out-of-scope work;
- a localized fix and lifecycle management;
- demand, activation, adoption, and continuity;
- actionable decisions in dashboards and the causal chain of value;
- positioning, acquisition, ethical choice, and monetization.

## Coverage produced by the agent

- `ADO-046` through `ADO-058`: product structure and features;
- `ADO-095` through `ADO-101`: differentiation, trust, and delivery in products
  created or accelerated with AI.

Every entry uses a strength, trigger, decision, evidence, and application boundary.
