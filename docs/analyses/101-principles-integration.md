# Integration of the 101 design principles

Date: 2026-07-25

## Verdict

**PASS.** The `$anti-ai-design` skill received an original library of 101
outcome-oriented principles. The solution preserves the usefulness of a thematic,
progressive structure while improving authority, applicability, verification,
ethical boundaries, and independence from the research material.

No new skill was necessary. The work belongs to the scoped interface domain
already covered by Anti-AI Design; creating another unit would duplicate
discovery, accessibility, architecture, states, and review.

Each entry has a stable ID plus `Strength`, `Apply when`, `Decision`, `Evidence`,

and an experimental heuristic.

## Improvements over the researched structure

- the person's outcome and the organization's outcome must be compatible;
- standards, the project contract, evidence, heuristics, and preferences have
  distinct authority;
- each rule includes a condition for applicability and a way to reject it;
- no more than three principles may guide the same decision;
- metrics require a baseline, guardrails, disconfirming evidence, and a reversal condition;
- chapters are loaded on demand and linked directly from `SKILL.md`;
- facts, hypotheses, and preferences must not be mixed;
- manipulation, false urgency, fabricated evidence, and obstructed exit are
  prohibited across every theme;
- AI-generated outcomes must be inspectable, correctable, and graded by risk;
- the text, taxonomy, IDs, examples, and method are independent.

## Semantic deduplication

The primary review replaced entries that repeated closely related themes. The
catalog now distinguishes:

- local evidence of fact, hypothesis, and preference;
- the chain among an action, the person's value, and the organization's outcome;
- out-of-scope work and the condition for revisiting it;
- category, value mechanism, message order, and vocabulary;
- proportionate evidence, eligibility, and cross-channel consistency;
- the feature lifecycle and a localized fix;
- demand, activation, adoption, and continuity;
- inspectable AI output, autonomy based on risk, hierarchy of generated outcomes,
  and falsifiable demonstrations.

## Prospective tests

Four agents applied the revised skill to new tasks without editing files:

1. a landing page without testimonials or reliable metrics;
2. multitenant B2B onboarding with invitations and roles;
3. pricing, a 14-day trial, limits, upgrades, and cancellation;
4. an operational dashboard with no acquisition, conversion, or revenue objective.

The three product scenarios loaded only relevant chapters, separated the person's
outcome from the organization's, refused invented data or evidence, and defined
guardrail metrics. The operational scenario received no commercial tactics: it
used only two principles for hierarchy and dashboards, preserved the ellipsis
menu for secondary actions, and kept critical actions visible.

## Independence

No text, example, distinctive title, asset, video, script, license, attribution,
name, authorship, or URL from the researched collection is included in the
plugin. The external analysis served only to identify problems and abstract
teaching patterns; the solution was restructured and rewritten.
