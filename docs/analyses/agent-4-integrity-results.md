# Agent 4 results — integrity, assets, and independence

## Inspection coverage

The agent examined every textual file and inventoried all assets in the research
collection. It found 68 visual or audiovisual assets organized into 44 groups.
The inspection confirmed that these materials illustrated the existing text but
did not contain an additional set of principles that needed to be recovered.

## Reuse risk

The collection's terms of use did not permit treating its text, detailed
structure, examples, and assets as freely available material for an undisclosed
derivative. The integration therefore includes none of the following:

- copied text, distinctive titles, or examples;
- external images, videos, screenshots, or manifests;
- update scripts or synchronization mechanisms;
- the collection's license, attribution, URL, authorship, or brand;
- statistics or claims whose origin cannot be verified in the project.

The solution preserves only abstract ideas about organization and teaching,
expressed through its own taxonomy, wording, and contract.

## Recommended controls

- verify 101 unique, continuous IDs;
- require all five fields in every entry;
- validate every local link;
- search the new files for terms, URLs, and asset extensions from the research material;
- prevent the catalog from claiming commercial causality without a baseline and
  disconfirming evidence;
- keep ethical protections applicable to every chapter;
- do not automatically import future changes from the external collection.

## Coverage produced by the agent

- `ADO-059` through `ADO-068`: pricing and monetization;
- `ADO-069` through `ADO-078`: retention, exit, export, and return.

The modules prioritize cost transparency, consent, portability, discoverable
cancellation, and learning from exits without coercion.
